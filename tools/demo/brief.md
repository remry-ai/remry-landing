# New checkout brief

## Scope

One-page checkout for web and mobile, with Apple Pay at launch and saved cards for signed-in buyers (PRD). Guest checkout stays as it is (PRD; Linear agrees).

## Timeline

Launch is **12 Nov** (PRD). Linear has the last milestone due **30 Oct**, and 14 of 22 issues are done. The payments API v2 cut-over has to land first (Linear).

## Pricing

Three tiers, Starter, Growth and Scale, with card fees passed through at cost (pricing sheet; the PRD agrees).

## Open questions

- Who owns fraud review after launch (PRD).
- Whether saved cards need a second consent screen in the EU (Linear).

## Discrepancies

- **Launch date.** PRD (edited 3 Nov): 12 Nov. Linear (updated 5 Nov): last milestone 30 Oct. The project's target date: 1 Dec.
- **Apple Pay.** PRD: at launch. Linear: in the follow-up milestone.
